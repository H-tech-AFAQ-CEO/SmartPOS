SmartMart POS

Privacy Policy • Last updated: November 2, 2026

Overview

SmartMart POS ("the app") is an offline billing, point-of-sale and inventory app. Your privacy matters. The app works entirely on your device and does not require an account or an internet connection.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or business data. The app has no user accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you enter is stored locally on your phone and never leaves it unless you choose to export or share it:

  • Shop profile (name, owner, phone, address, tax number, receipt footer)
  • Products, stock levels, purchases and suppliers
  • Sales, invoices, returns and expenses
  • Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, ledger (Khata) and loyalty points
  • Shift and cash drawer records
  • App settings and optional PIN

Data is kept in a local SQLite database and app preferences on your device. You are the sole controller of this data.

Sharing & Exports

The app lets you create PDF receipts, CSV reports, customer statements and backup files. These are created only when you ask, and are shared only through the share sheet or print dialog of your device to the destination you pick (for example WhatsApp, email, Google Drive or a printer). We do not receive these files.

Backup & Restore

Backups are copies of your database saved on your device. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it if you lose your phone or uninstall the app without a backup. Please create backups regularly and store them somewhere safe.

Permissions

  • File access: used only when you choose a backup file to restore.
  • Printing & sharing: used only when you print or share a receipt, report or backup.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

PIN Lock & Security

You may set an optional 4-digit PIN to lock the app. The PIN is stored on your device only. Keep your device secure, as anyone with access to it and the app may view the stored data.

Customer Data You Enter

If you store information about your own customers, you are responsible for handling it in line with the laws that apply to your business. We never see this information.

Children's Privacy

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete records inside the app, or remove all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Backup files you created or shared must be deleted separately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with an updated date.
